- The daily trends record is written to a private file beside `trends.jsonl`, saved to disk, then renamed over it, so a crash, a power cut or a full disk mid-write can't cut the history short. If the file can't be read, that day's record is skipped instead of starting the history over. A failed trends write no longer stops the 2-hourly vitals check before its low-disk warning, and it's noted in the journal.

- **`cos net`**: is it the Wi-Fi, the router or the internet? A few seconds, one plain
  verdict ("Network looks fine", "Wi-Fi signal is weak (-78 dBm)…", "A sign-in page is
  waiting…"), then the signal, the router, the internet and DNS. It never reads the network's
  name or anything else that identifies where you are, and sends nothing to your local
  network. `cos net --speed` measures throughput with macOS's own networkQuality, only when
  you ask ([0010](docs/decisions/0010-net.md)).

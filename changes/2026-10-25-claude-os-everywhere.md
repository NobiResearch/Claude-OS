- **Claude OS everywhere.** `/music`, `/tidy`, `/update` and `/startup` work in Claude Code from
  any folder on your Mac: `cos install` links them into `~/.claude/skills`, and they load live
  from Claude OS. Their descriptions keep them out of your projects' own dependencies, code
  clean-ups and video music, and nothing about the project you're in gets recorded. `cos
  uninstall` removes the links ([0009](docs/decisions/0009-claude-os-everywhere.md)).

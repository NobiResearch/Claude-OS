- **Keep Claude OS up to date** (Settings › Permissions, off until you turn it on in the
  app's own dialog): what you merge on GitHub comes to this Mac by itself, when the app
  starts, when a terminal session starts, and while the app is closed. While the app is
  open, **Update** in its menu bar restarts just its server onto the new code; music keeps
  playing. `cos config` can no longer turn Night shift or this on; only the dialog can
  ([0011](docs/decisions/0011-catching-up-with-main.md)).

  bodies, comments and reviews, issues, release notes, and `gh api` fields. A problem (your home
  path, a secret, your email) stops the command, and says what kind of problem without repeating
  it. A body file has to come from the repository or the temp folder, never a private one. Your
  own `gh` stays card-free: a stop goes back to Claude, never to you.
